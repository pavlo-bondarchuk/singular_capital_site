About Singular Capital

A personal approach to real estate.

Singular Capital is an independent boutique advisory firm focused on international real estate opportunities and transaction coordination.

We work with buyers, property owners, developers and potential financing partners to identify opportunities and coordinate transactions shaped around the needs of the parties involved.

Our approach

We value direct relationships, careful consideration of the asset, clear communication and a tailored process. We do not assume that one transaction structure or strategy fits every client.

International perspective

We consider opportunities across markets, including Europe, the United States and the Middle East, subject to mandate, local requirements and the availability of appropriate professional support.

Our role

Our role in each engagement is defined individually and confirmed in writing. Depending on the mandate, it may include sourcing, introductions, advisory support and transaction coordination. Legal, tax, engineering, valuation and regulated financial services should be provided by appropriately qualified independent professionals where required.
