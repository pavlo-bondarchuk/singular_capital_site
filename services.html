Services

Real estate advisory & transaction support.

01

Property sourcing and acquisition support

We help clients define acquisition criteria and identify potential properties, which may include completed assets, properties under development or assets with renovation, redevelopment or repositioning potential.

02

Transaction coordination

We facilitate communication between transaction parties and coordinate practical next steps. The scope of services, authority and compensation are agreed in writing for each mandate.

03

Private capital introductions

Where appropriate and legally permissible, we may introduce project sponsors or property businesses to potential financing counterparties and coordinate discussions. We do not hold client funds. Any financing is subject to separate agreements between the relevant parties, due diligence, approvals and applicable law.

04

Development and value enhancement

We help clients explore potential renovation, redevelopment, change-of-use and other asset strategies. No outcome, approval, timetable, rental income or capital appreciation is guaranteed.

05

Project-specific structures

Where a transaction may benefit from a dedicated entity, we can coordinate with independent legal, tax and corporate professionals. Singular Capital does not provide legal or tax advice.

The term “private capital” describes our transaction focus and does not mean that we operate an investment fund, offer securities, provide regulated investment advice or arrange securities transactions in any jurisdiction where we are not authorized to do so.
