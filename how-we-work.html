How we work

A clear process, tailored to the mandate.

01 — Understand the brief

We discuss objectives, property preferences, geography, timing and the desired role of the client.

02 — Confirm scope

If there is a potential fit, we clarify the proposed services, responsibilities, fees, conflicts and any limitations in a written engagement before work begins.

03 — Identify and coordinate

We identify potential opportunities or relevant counterparties and coordinate communications and next steps within the agreed scope.

04 — Independent due diligence

Clients should appoint suitable independent legal, tax, technical, valuation and other advisers. Any decision remains subject to their own assessment and contractual documentation.

05 — Transaction completion

The parties execute definitive agreements and transfer funds directly between themselves or through an independently appointed closing professional, as agreed for the transaction. Singular Capital does not receive or hold client funds under its stated intermediary model.

The sequence may vary by transaction. No mandate is accepted until confirmed in writing.
