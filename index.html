Our perspective

Independent thinking. Direct relationships.

Every property and every client objective is different. We take a personal, relationship-driven approach to sourcing, evaluating and coordinating real estate transactions—whether the opportunity involves an existing asset, a development project or the repositioning of a property.

Our role is to bring the relevant parties together, support the transaction process and coordinate with appropriate independent professionals where needed.

A tailored process

One mandate at a time.

We begin by understanding the client’s objectives, criteria, timeline and preferred level of involvement. We then assess whether a potential mandate is a fit, clarify our role and fees in writing, and coordinate next steps with the relevant parties.
