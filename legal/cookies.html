Cookie Policy

Draft — publish only after checking the production website.

1. Current prototype

This site does not intentionally install analytics or advertising cookies. When you use the contact form, PHP sets a singular_contact session cookie to support the form’s security and rate limiting. It lasts for the browser session and is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax; Secure is enabled when the site uses HTTPS. The hosting platform, security layer or embedded services may also use cookies or similar technologies. The production site must be scanned before this policy is finalized.

2. Types of cookies

Essential cookies may be needed for security, network operation or features explicitly requested by a visitor. Analytics, personalization and advertising technologies should not be enabled until their purpose, providers, retention and consent requirements have been assessed.

3. Managing preferences

Browser settings can be used to block or delete cookies, although some site functions may then not work. Where required, a consent interface must allow visitors to accept, reject and manage non-essential cookies with equal clarity.
