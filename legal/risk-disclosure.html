Real Estate Risk Disclosure

TermsPrivacyCookiesDisclaimerRisks

Draft disclosure for use alongside transaction-specific documents.

Real estate is illiquid and values can fall. A buyer may be unable to sell at the desired time or price. Development and renovation projects may face cost overruns, contractor disputes, delays, permitting restrictions, environmental issues, title defects or changes in market demand. Rental income can be interrupted by vacancies, non-payment, repairs, taxes, regulation or changes in operating costs. Cross-border transactions can involve currency movements, different legal systems, local ownership restrictions, tax exposure and enforcement challenges. Financing may not be available when expected, and a counterparty may fail to perform.

Any projected yield, resale value, budget, timetable or capital appreciation is an estimate only unless expressly guaranteed in a binding written agreement by a party legally able to give that guarantee. Singular Capital does not guarantee any outcome. Clients should obtain independent professional advice and review all transaction documents before committing funds.
